# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, accessible, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, respectful,
healthy, and public-interest community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect for different experiences and uses of data;
- giving and accepting specific, evidence-based feedback;
- using plain language and explaining specialized terms;
- acknowledging uncertainty, mistakes, sources, and contributors;
- prioritizing community safety, privacy, accessibility, and informed reuse;
- focusing disagreement on evidence and consequences, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized conduct,
insults, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private information,
deliberately misleading data claims, sustained disruption, or retaliation
against a person who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
events, meetings, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner through the private
contact method listed in the GitHub organization or repository profile. Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive details. If no project-private channel
is available or an owner is involved, use GitHub's platform reporting tools and
ask an uninvolved organization owner to review the matter.

Project leaders will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible,
avoid conflicts of interest, document the basis for action, and prohibit
retaliation. Depending on severity and history, consequences may include a
private correction, warning, temporary participation restriction, removal of
content or access, or permanent exclusion. Immediate threats or credible safety
risks may be acted on before a full review.

A person subject to an enforcement decision may request review by an
organization owner who was not involved in the original decision.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted to the
project's public-data context.
